## Slide 1 / Page 1: Understand the Student

### 1. Who Exactly Are We Targeting?
* **Core Persona:** Final-year B.Tech / B.E. students (Batch of 2025/2026) across Tier-2 and Tier-3 engineering colleges (e.g., SRM AP, VIT-AP, KL University, Vignan, JNTU affiliates).
* **Branches:** 
  * 60% CSE / IT / Data Science (Primary audience).
  * 40% ECE / EEE / Mechanical / Civil (The untapped growth lever).
* **Current Mindset:**
  * **Placement Anxiety:** Tech hiring slowdowns have left them anxious. Recruiters actively filter out standard resumes with outdated academic projects (e.g., basic weather apps or library management systems).
  * **The AI Gap:** They know AI is mandatory, but feel overwhelmed by heavy theory, math, or complex neural networks.

### 2. Why Would They Care?
* **Instant Gratification:** “Zero theory fluff — build and deploy a working AI project to GitHub in 60 minutes.”
* **Direct Placement Value:** A tangible bullet point to put on their resume for upcoming placement drives.
* **Low Barrier to Entry:** Free + beginner-friendly + zero prior ML experience required.

### 3. What Makes Them Register?
* **Proof of Output:** Seeing the exact end project before registering (e.g., *“Build an AI Resume Screener”*).
* **Peer Influence & FOMO:** Seeing classmates and batchmates from their own section/branch registering.
* **College Rivalry:** Knowing their college is competing on a leaderboard against rival universities.

## Slide 2: The Core Marketing Strategy & Prioritized Channels
> **Core Constraint Analysis:** A ₹2,000 budget cannot buy 500 registrations via paid ads. At an average CPC of ₹35–₹50 on Meta/Google Ads, ₹2,000 yields only 40–55 clicks (~15–20 registrations).  
> **Strategic Pivot:** Instead of buying traffic, we turn registered students into organic distributors via **incentivized peer loops and campus micro-champions**.

```
[Day 1-2: Seed Champions] ──► 10 College Groups & CRs ──► 200 Initial Registrations
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
[Day 3-5: BuildRush Loop] ◄── Referral Link & Leaderboard ◄───┘ (K-factor = 1.15)
           │
           ▼
     230 Peer Referrals
           │
           ▼
[Day 6-7: Campus War FOMO] ──► Inter-College Battle & Scarcity Push ──► 90 Registrations
                                                                        ──────
                                                               Total:   520 Registrations
```

### Prioritized Channels (Only 2 Core Channels):
1. **Channel 1: WhatsApp Class Representatives (CRs) & Tech Club Leads (The Seed)**
   * **Action:** Direct outreach to 10–12 influential CRs and club leads across 5 colleges.
   * **Incentive:** ₹200 Amazon voucher per CR who drives 30+ verified signups from their branch (₹800 allocated for 4 top performers).
   * **Why it works:** Students open 98% of WhatsApp messages from their batch group; CR recommendations carry 10x higher trust than ads.

2. **Channel 2: The "BuildRush" Viral Referral Loop (The Multiplier)**
   * **Action:** Every registrant immediately receives a unique referral code and personalized WhatsApp pitch generated by our built asset.
   * **Incentive:** Top 5 student referrers win ₹200 mobile recharge each (₹1,000 prize pool).
   * **Why it works:** Gamification, real cash stakes within student budget reality, and 1-tap WhatsApp sharing.

## Slide 4: Funnel Math & Budget Allocation

$$\text{Total Registrations} = \text{Seed Signups} + \text{Referral Signups} + \text{Urgency Surge}$$

1. **Seed Registrations (Days 1–2):**  
   10 colleges $\times$ 1 active CR/Club lead $\times$ 20 registrations/group = **200 students**.
2. **Referral Multiplier (Days 3–5):**  
   With the ₹1,000 cash incentive and leaderboard mechanics, average viral coefficient $K = 1.15$:  
   $200 \times 1.15 =$ **230 students**.
3. **Campus War & Urgency Push (Days 6–7):**
