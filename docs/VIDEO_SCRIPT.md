## 🎬 Section-by-Section Script

### **[0:00 – 0:40] The Problem & The Student Insight**
*(Camera on face. Confident, energetic, direct tone.)*

> *"Hi, I'm Bharadwaj Bollapalli, and this is my submission for the NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge.*
> 
> *Our goal is to get **500 final-year engineering students** to register for the free workshop: **'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'** in 7 days with a budget of just **₹2,000**.*
> 
> *When looking at final-year students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges right now, one thing is clear: **Campus placement anxiety is at an all-time high.** With tech hiring freezes, generic resumes with basic weather apps get instantly rejected by recruiters. They desperately want an AI project on their resume, but feel overwhelmed by heavy theory.*
> 
> *Our core hook is simple: **Zero theory. Walk in with no AI experience, walk out with a live, deployed AI project on your GitHub in 60 minutes.**"*

---

### **[0:40 – 1:25] The Strategy: Why Paid Ads Fail on ₹2,000**
*(Transition to screen share or keep talking directly to camera.)*

> *"Now, looking at the budget constraint: **₹2,000 cannot buy 500 registrations via Meta or Google ads.** At a ₹40 CPC, ₹2,000 only gets us about 50 clicks, which converts to at most 15 registrations.*
> 
> *So instead of buying traffic, I turned the budget into a **viral growth loop**:*
> 1. *We use **₹800 to partner with Class Representatives (CRs)** and tech club leads across 5 colleges to seed our initial 200 registrations in official WhatsApp groups.*
> 2. *We use **₹1,000 as a prize pool** where the **Top 5 student referrers win a ₹200 mobile recharge each**.*
> 3. *And to make this loop frictionless, I built **BuildRush** — a dedicated referral engine that turns every registered student into an organic distribution channel."*

---

### **[1:25 – 2:20] Live Asset Walkthrough (BuildRush Demo)**
*(Screen share of: `https://bollapallibharadwaj.github.io/BuildRush/`)*

> *"Here is the working asset I built, deployed live on GitHub Pages.*
> 
> *First, we collect the student's name, their 10-digit WhatsApp number for workshop link delivery, and their engineering branch.*
> 
> *When they click register — notice the celebratory confetti — they immediately get their unique referral code and enter their dashboard.*
> 
> *Now, here is the **AI personalization hook**: non-CSE students in Mechanical or ECE often ignore AI workshops assuming it's only for coders. In BuildRush, when an ECE student registers, the tool automatically matches them with an **Edge AI project**, and if they're Mechanical, a **Predictive Maintenance project**, and generates a tailored WhatsApp invite across 4 distinct tones — from Placement impact to Urgent FOMO.*
> 
> *With one tap on **'Share on WhatsApp'**, it deep-links directly into WhatsApp with zero API cost.*
> 
> *To gamify this, we have a live progress bar toward the 500 milestone, an **Inter-College Battle** between rival universities like SRM and VIT, and a **Top Referrers Leaderboard**.*
> 
> *(Click the demo button)* *And for evaluators, I built this simulation button so you can test live referral counting and leaderboard updates in literally 3 seconds."*

---

### **[2:20 – 3:00] AI Learnings & What I Rejected**
*(Camera back on face. Clear, reflective tone.)*

> *"Lastly, how I used AI and where I exercised human judgment:*
> 
> *When I first prompted AI for a marketing plan, it repeatedly suggested running Instagram Story ads and building a complex web app with JWT authentication and passwords.*
> 
> *I deliberately **rejected both suggestions**:*
> * *I rejected paid ads because the math simply doesn't work on a ₹2,000 budget.*
> * *And I rejected passwords and email verification because unnecessary friction kills viral sign-ups. I replaced it with a clean 10-digit mobile capture, an embedded SQLite database, and zero-setup browser storage.*
> 
> *This challenge showed me that tools like Gemini and ChatGPT are fantastic for accelerating code and brainstorming, but true growth comes from **understanding user psychology, respecting budget constraints, and having the bias to ship something real.***
> 
> *Thank you, and I look forward to the interview!"*
